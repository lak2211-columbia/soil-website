SOIL · Identity, round three

Wordmark studies

Sketches for a hand-drawn SOIL mark, made in code so we can judge structure, scale and colour before anyone picks up a brush. Round three refines the traced serif you picked; rounds two and one follow it. The finished mark should be drawn by hand on paper, then traced.

Under each study, the mark is shown at the size it takes in the site header, on the Porcelain and Ink palettes, and as a browser-tab icon at 32 and 16 pixels. A mark that only works large is a cover, not a logo.

Next

From sketch to mark

  1. Print the skeletonSet “soil” in Cormorant at about 300 point and print a few sheets on thin paper.
  2. Trace it many timesTwenty or thirty passes with the tool of the direction you pick: brush, fine pen, or a carving knife for the seal. By the tenth pass you stop copying and start drawing.
  3. Judge it smallScan the best three at 1200 dpi. Look at them at tab-icon size before you look at them large.
  4. Trace to vectorOne colour, clean paths, no texture baked in, so the mark can follow the palette. Test at 16 pixels before anything else.
