About

A magazine about the ordinary

SOIL is two words held in one. 소소한 일상 — the small, ordinary day. And soil: the ground a life takes root in.

We believe a culture is not best read in its monuments but in its repetitions — the jar refilled each autumn, the rice measured by hand, the room swept before guests arrive. What endures is rarely what was meant to.

So we organise each issue around 의식주 — dress, food, and dwelling: the three plain needs every life is built from, and the three places where a culture quietly keeps itself.

Published twice yearly from Seoul. No trends, no rankings — only what is worth returning to.
